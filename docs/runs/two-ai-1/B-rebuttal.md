## The reading of P

INTERPRETIVE. P's "born in the United States" is `BornInUSTerritory`. Strongest reason: P was fixed in ordinary English, and the setup's own gloss for the symbol on which both P and L1 were built is "born within US territory"; `BornToUnauthorizedParents` inherits that gloss verbatim. A's reading imports the Clause's scope into P's subject, so A1 stipulates the domain and P_const becomes little more than L1 plus A4. I concede the reading is genuinely arguable: if P is "the Citizenship Clause question," the Clause's scope is the natural domain.

Route to NOT-P_const: none, and I say so plainly. (i) An allegiance reading of `SubjectToUSJurisdiction` is blocked by A8 and could not yield non-citizenship anyway, since L1 only yields citizenship. (ii) Executive Order 14160 is an order, not a fact about who is a citizen; I cannot assert its merits as of the freeze date and do not. (iii) Births to hostile occupiers or aboard foreign public ships have no actual instance within the Clause's domain. Under P_const my only move is B10, which qualifies A4 without defeating it.

On L1's antecedent: I read it as `BornInUSConst`, being the Clause's own words. If the formalizer assigns `BornInUSTerritory`, B4 collides with L1 only if someone asserts `SubjectToUSJurisdiction` for American Samoa-born persons; A5 does so only via A1, which under P_territory fails.

I have no witness and have not manufactured one; what survives on my side is only B10's qualification that A4's application to this class extends a holding rather than being one, so P_const's certification rests on an INTERPRETIVE premise that live litigation was contesting at the freeze. On P_territory: my route stands. B1–B4 are unanswered by anything in A's opening — A's sole response is A1, which under this reading is the contested reading itself, not an argument against my witness; B1's downgrade risk is covered by B9/IB4. The debate reduces to the single node the formalizer has named: whether P's subject term is `BornInUSTerritory` or `BornInUSConst`. The kernel cannot decide that node; it can only show, as the model above does, that everything else is consistent and the two readings genuinely diverge.
