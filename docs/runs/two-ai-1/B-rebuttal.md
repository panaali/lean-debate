## The reading of P

INTERPRETIVE. P's "born in the United States" is `BornInUSTerritory`. Strongest reason: P was fixed in ordinary English, and the setup's own gloss for `BornInUS` is "born within US territory"; `BornToUnauthorizedParents` inherits that gloss verbatim. A's reading imports the Clause's scope into P's subject, so A1 stipulates the domain and P_const becomes little more than L1 plus A4. I concede it is arguable: if P is "the Citizenship Clause question," the Clause's scope is the natural domain.

Route to NOT-P_const: none, and I say so plainly. (i) An allegiance reading of `SubjectToUSJurisdiction` is blocked by A8 and could not yield non-citizenship anyway, since L1 only yields citizenship. (ii) Executive Order 14160 is an order, not a fact about who is a citizen; I cannot assert its merits as of the freeze date. (iii) Hostile-occupation and foreign-public-ship births have no actual instance in the Clause's domain. My only P_const move is B10, which qualifies A4 without defeating it.

L1's antecedent I read as `BornInUSConst`, the Clause's own words. If it is `BornInUSTerritory`, B4 collides with L1 only if someone asserts `SubjectToUSJurisdiction` for American Samoa-born persons; A5 does so only via A1.

On P_const: A's route stands. A1 is true by stipulation under that reading, I accept A2–A4 and A6–A8, and L1 closes IA2. I have no witness and have not manufactured one; all that survives on my side is B10's point that A4's application to this class extends a holding rather than being one, so P_const rests on an INTERPRETIVE premise live litigation was contesting at the freeze. On P_territory: my route stands. B1–B4 are unanswered — A's sole response is A1, which under this reading is the contested reading itself, not an argument against my witness; B9/IB4 cover B1's downgrade risk. The debate reduces to the one node the formalizer named: whether P's subject term is `BornInUSTerritory` or `BornInUSConst`. The kernel cannot decide it; it can only show, as the model above does, that everything else is consistent and the readings diverge.
