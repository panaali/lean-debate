Yes—the missing piece is **an iterative challenge-and-revision loop**, rather than a single self-check at the end.

The process should be:

1. **Form a provisional interpretation** grounded in the evidence.

3. **Challenge your understanding:** Did you misread wording, omit context, confuse different claims, or assume intent?
4. **Identify what would distinguish competing interpretations**, then seek that evidence.
5. **Revise the conclusion and probabilities**, and repeat.

But **stability is not proof of accuracy**. Repeatedly reconsidering the same evidence can reinforce a mistake.
